## Who is this app for?

Maintenance planner or operations engineer preparing a work package for field technicians. Works at a desk in Fusion. Knows the work-order domain and does not author Industrial Canvas by hand.

## What problem does this solve?

Without this template, engineers manually locate a work order, browse related files and time series, open Industrial Canvas, and place resources one by one. That takes about 30 minutes and depends on knowing which CDF data exists.

## Tasks and success

**One-sentence story.** As a maintenance planner, I want a guided flow that turns a work order into a complete Industrial Canvas work package so that I can publish a consistent package in under 5 minutes.

**Success criteria.** Time to produce a complete Industrial Canvas work package drops from about 30 minutes of manual authoring to under 5 minutes.
